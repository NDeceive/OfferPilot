# Competitor research brief

No verified competitor set has been approved yet.

Before future logo work, record for each relevant competitor:

- Product category and target audience
- Logo type and dominant silhouette
- Color and typography conventions
- Common category cliches
- Visual territory OfferPilot should avoid or differentiate from
- Source URL and observation date

Do not copy competitor marks into the asset library. Screenshots or links are research evidence only and may carry copyright and trademark restrictions.
