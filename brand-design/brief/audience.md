# Audience brief

## Primary audience

Students and early-career candidates preparing for specific roles, often under time pressure before an application or interview.

## Needs

- A clear next action rather than a dense dashboard.
- Credible, specific guidance tied to their role, resume, and practice history.
- Readable Chinese interfaces on mobile and desktop.
- Encouragement without exaggerated promises or technological theater.
- Honest empty, loading, error, and recovery states.

## Accessibility constraints

- Touch targets of at least 44 px.
- Keyboard focus support.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Sufficient text contrast.
- No truncation or horizontal scrolling at 360-430 px.
