# Product brief

OfferPilot / 智面幻境 is a Vue 3 web product for students and early-career candidates. It links target roles, resumes, focused practice, simulated interviews, records, and feedback into a continuous training loop.

The desired outcome is not passive content consumption. Users should quickly understand their current state, start the next useful practice, and see honest evidence of progress.

Primary surfaces include desktop web, 360-430 px mobile web, and a future Capacitor Android WebView.
