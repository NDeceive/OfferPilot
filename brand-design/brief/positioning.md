# Positioning brief

OfferPilot turns a user's real target role, resume, and past interview feedback into the next useful training action. It is not an isolated generic question bank and should not present itself as an AI spectacle.

## Provisional personality

- Practical, not bureaucratic
- Warm, not cute
- Credible, not grandiose
- Focused, not sparse for its own sake
- Modern, not trend-chasing

## Visual implication

Favor calm hierarchy, purposeful contrast, restrained color, clear typography, and recognizable geometry. Avoid neon cyber aesthetics, decorative AI symbolism, and effects that mask weak structure.
