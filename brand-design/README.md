# OfferPilot brand design workspace

This directory is the controlled workspace for future brand and logo work. Environment setup does not authorize changing the current product logo.

- `brief/`: product and strategy evidence.
- `references/`: curated links or files for structural study only.
- `assets/`: adopted fonts, icons, and illustrations with licenses.
- `explorations/`: dated or round-based concept work.
- `tests/`: neutral application and small-size tests.
- `final/`: approved, production-ready assets only.
- `ASSET_LICENSES.md`: mandatory provenance and license ledger.

Do not place an asset in `final/` until it passes `logo-visual-review` and its license/provenance is recorded.
