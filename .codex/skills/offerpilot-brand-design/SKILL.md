# OfferPilot Brand Design

Read the repository's `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and [references/constraints.md](references/constraints.md) before making OfferPilot visual decisions. Treat current implementation as evidence, not automatic approval of every legacy style.

## Product truth

OfferPilot / 智面幻境 helps students and early-career candidates connect target roles, resumes, practice, simulated interviews, and feedback into the next useful training action. The tone is practical, warm, credible, and action-oriented. Do not invent outcomes, customers, scores, testimonials, or AI claims.

## Design priorities

- Clarity and calm confidence before technological spectacle.
- Product value before an AI symbol.
- Strong hierarchy, Chinese readability, low cognitive load, and honest states.
- Mobile operation in 360-430 px and desktop use; touch targets at least 44 px.
- A logo must work in black and white before color or motion.

## Logo constraints

Design symbol and wordmark separately. Prioritize geometry, negative space, proportion, silhouette, recognition, memorability, and optical balance. Test 16, 32, and 64 px, app icon, navbar, slides, poster, dark/light backgrounds, and one-color print.

Avoid hexagonal tech marks, AI brains, robot heads, circuits, glowing particles, sparkles, unexplained gradient orbs, infinity symbols, forced letter combinations, stock-icon marks, and decorative noise. If removing gradient, glow, shadow, or 3D breaks the mark, reject the route.

Do not alter the current product logo unless the user explicitly asks. When they do, use `logo-design` and its review gates.

## Project records

Keep briefs, references, explorations, tests, finals, and license records under `brand-design/`. Every adopted external asset must be recorded in `brand-design/ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
